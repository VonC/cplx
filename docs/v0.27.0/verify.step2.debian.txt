change matters most. Here the fallback is not an override being honoured; it is

mirror` passing at exit 7 is the Q19 defect being removed, measured on the host

mirror and a prefix with nothing deployed: the defect, written down. It now
asserts exit 7 at the root-file site under the `mirrored-not-deployed` oracle,

3 must re-point this case again rather than delete it.
