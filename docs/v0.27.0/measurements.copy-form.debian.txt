================ measurement answers ================
One matrix per form over all eight destination shapes, every shape present
whatever its outcome. "exit" is the status of the form as a whole, which
for F3 is the mv step unless the copy step already failed. "after" is what
prefix/name is once the form returned. "nested" says whether the copy
landed inside a directory destination instead of replacing it. "tmp" says
whether F3 left its temporary behind. "bytes" and "mtime" are the external
symlink target, read separately so a rewrite with identical content still
shows up. "ms" is elapsed time, which is how a prompt refusal is told from
a block. A cell reads n/a when the shape cannot raise that question,
unavailable when the tool or option is missing here, skipped when the case
was withheld as unsafe, and n/r when no record was produced.

--- the four deciding cells ---
The review named these: does the external target survive byte-identical
when the destination is a symlink to a regular file, and does the form
refuse or replace promptly instead of blocking on a FIFO.
